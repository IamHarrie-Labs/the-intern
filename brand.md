# Brand — The Intern

_Status: active_

The Intern has two connected visual modes. The landing screen behaves like a hand-drawn corporate orientation film. The playable interface uses the original clean office system so controls, measurements, and outcomes remain easy to read.

## Game palette

- Paper: `#F3EBDD`
- Ink: `#19201D`
- Muted ink: `#5D655F`
- Office green: `#1F6B4F`
- Safety orange: `#E9652C`
- Failure red: `#A93F32`
- Grid line: `#CFC4B3`

## Landing palette

- Paper: `#F4F3EF`
- Ink: `#14140F`
- Warning red: `#C0342A`
- Sensor blue: `#9ABEEF`
- Attention yellow: `#F2C31A`

## Typography

- Landing display and buttons: Luckiest Guy with Nunito supporting copy.
- Game UI: IBM Plex Sans.
- Game measurements and system labels: IBM Plex Mono.

## Motion

The opening behaves like a short orientation film built in live HTML and CSS: drifting institutional type, rotating target ring, bobbing Intern, falling scores, and stacks of evaluation forms. Motion should feel physical and handmade. Reduced-motion preferences collapse all loops to near-instant states.

## Voice

The Intern is earnest and literal. Interface copy is brief. Jokes come from bureaucratic confidence colliding with the physical outcome.
